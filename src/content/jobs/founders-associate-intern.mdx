## Role profile

A Founders Associate Intern works across strategy, go-to-market, sales operations, automation, revenue analysis and day-to-day execution. The role is intentionally broad and suits someone who is comfortable moving between research, commercial work, operations and AI-assisted automation.

This page is retained as a ZebraByte Careers role profile. It describes a type of role and the skills associated with it; it is not currently represented as an open ZebraByte vacancy unless the Careers page explicitly marks it as open.

## The Role

You work close to company leadership across the work required to keep an early-stage technology business moving: strategy, go-to-market, sales operations, automation, revenue tracking and operational problem-solving.

One day you may be shaping a pitch deck, the next building an automation to improve a sales flow, and the next investigating why a business metric does not reconcile.

## What You'll Do

- **Strategy:** Support market research, competitive analysis and evaluation of new opportunities.
- **Go-to-market:** Work hands-on with outbound, campaigns, messaging, sales operations and experiments.
- **Automation & Sales Ops:** Build workflows, AI agents and automations that reduce repetitive work.
- **Revenue tracking:** Help keep pipeline, deals, recurring revenue and renewals measurable and understandable.
- **Operations:** Take ownership of practical problems that need to be solved to keep the business moving.

## What We're Looking For

You're likely to fit this type of role if you:

- Enjoy technology and are genuinely curious about how systems and businesses work.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, learn quickly and keep moving.
- Are comfortable in an early-stage environment where not every process has a playbook.
- Understand the basics of AI agents, LLMs and automation and want to use them practically.
- Communicate clearly in writing and in conversation.

### Nice to Have

- Experience with a startup, side project or something you built and launched yourself.
- Practical comfort with AI-assisted research, coding or workflow automation.
- Interest in security, compliance, SaaS operations or technical B2B services.

## Working With AI

Modern operations roles increasingly use tools such as OpenAI, Codex, Cursor and other AI-assisted workflows to research, automate and ship faster. The important skill is not simply knowing a tool name, but knowing how to apply AI safely and productively to real work.

## Why This Role

- Learn how strategy, sales, operations and automation interact inside a technology business.
- Own real projects instead of observing from the sidelines.
- Develop commercial and operational judgment alongside technical automation skills.
- Work across functions and learn how decisions translate into measurable execution.

## Typical Evaluation Process

A future hiring process for this type of role may include:

1. **Intro call:** background, motivation and expectations.
2. **Case study:** a practical exercise involving research, operations or automation.
3. **Debrief:** discussion of decisions, trade-offs and communication.
