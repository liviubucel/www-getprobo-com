## Role profile

A Forward Deployed Engineer works directly at the intersection of customers, compliance, security and product. The role combines technical discovery, program implementation, evidence management, remediation and customer ownership.

This page is retained as a ZebraByte Careers role profile. It describes the type of work and experience relevant to this position; it is not currently represented as an open vacancy unless the Careers page explicitly marks it as open.

## The Role

You work directly with customers: assessing their compliance and security posture, building their compliance program, implementing the right controls in the platform, and helping them prepare for audit. You own the relationship end-to-end.

The role requires autonomy, technical judgment and the ability to translate real infrastructure into actionable compliance and security controls.

## What You'll Do

- **Assess:** Run discovery, map infrastructure and data flows against target frameworks, identify gaps, and build a realistic roadmap to compliance.
- **Build:** Design and implement compliance programs: policies, controls, risk registers, vendor assessments and evidence workflows tailored to each organization.
- **Ship through audit:** Prepare evidence, coordinate audit-readiness work and drive findings to closure.
- **Stay close:** Be a reliable technical point of contact. Respond quickly and flag risks before they become larger problems.
- **Automate:** Use AI tooling, integrations and workflow automation to eliminate repetitive work.
- **Shape the product:** Turn recurring customer problems into better platform workflows, documentation and product requirements.

## What We're Looking For

### Required

- 3+ years of experience in a technology environment.
- Technical background in engineering, security, architecture or technical support. You can inspect a cloud environment and translate it into actionable controls.
- Hands-on experience with at least one major compliance framework such as SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR or HIPAA.
- Strong automation mindset and practical use of AI or scripting to reduce manual work.
- Organized approach to managing several workstreams without losing ownership.
- Good judgment about when to investigate independently and when to escalate or ask for specialist input.
- Clear communication with CTOs, engineers, operational teams and auditors.

### Nice to Have

- Experience in information security, compliance or GRC in a hands-on role.
- Experience with cloud environments such as AWS, GCP or Azure and SaaS security tooling such as MDM, SIEM and SSO/IdP.
- Relevant certifications such as CISSP, CISA, ISO 27001 Lead Auditor or CCSP.
- Early-stage technology company experience.

## Why This Role

- Work at the intersection of technical product, security, compliance and customer outcomes.
- Solve different infrastructure and governance problems across organizations and industries.
- Own work end-to-end rather than handing every stage to another team.
- Influence product workflows directly through real customer and audit-readiness experience.
- Build automation around work that is traditionally repetitive and document-heavy.
